Multi-Stage Verification Architecture

Stage 1

Sieve & PRP Screening

Candidates of the form \(7^n - 2\) are sieved locally using modular arithmetic up to \(p = 10,000,000\), removing ~92% of composites.

Survivors pass to OpenPFGW for a 3-PRP test to confirm probable primality (error bound \(< 2^{-128}\)).

Stage 2

Deterministic ECPP

For moderate exponents, confirmed PRPs are run through Primo (v4.3.3 LX64) executing Elliptic Curve Primality Proving.

Primo outputs a 100% mathematically rigorous certificate (`.out`) independently verifiable with `verifier-f4`.

Stage 3

Ultra-Large MR Certification

When candidate digit lengths exceed practical ECPP compute limits, targets are certified to near-certainty via a multi-stage Miller-Rabin suite.

Evaluating \(k\) independent bases reduces composite error probability to under \(4^{-k}\) (e.g., \(1.6 \times 10^{-60}\) for \(k=100\)).
